# GATT Lab Privacy Policy

GATT Lab is the sample iOS app for [kmp-ble](https://github.com/gary-quinn/kmp-ble), a Kotlin Multiplatform Bluetooth Low Energy library.

## Data collection

GATT Lab does not collect, store, or transmit personal data to the developer or any third party.

## Bluetooth

The app uses Bluetooth on your device only to scan for and connect to nearby BLE peripherals. Bluetooth activity stays on-device and is not sent to external servers.

## Accounts and tracking

GATT Lab does not offer user accounts. It does not include analytics, advertising, or tracking.

## Data sharing

No data is sold or shared with third parties for marketing or profiling.

## Contact
