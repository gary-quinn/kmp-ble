# Privacy Policy - GATT Lab

GATT Lab is a sample application for the [kmp-ble](https://github.com/gary-quinn/kmp-ble) project.

## Data collection

GATT Lab does not collect, store, or transmit personal data to any server.

## Bluetooth

The app uses Bluetooth on your device to discover and communicate with nearby BLE devices. All Bluetooth activity stays on your device and is not sent to us or any third party.

## Accounts

GATT Lab does not require an account or sign-in.

## Analytics, ads, and tracking

GATT Lab does not use analytics, advertising, or tracking.

## Data sales

We do not sell your data.
